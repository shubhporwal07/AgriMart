## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a premium, trustworthy, and modern online grocery experience. It balances culinary indulgence with meticulous logistical reliability.

### Target Audience & Emotional Tone
Designed for discerning, quality-conscious consumers who view grocery shopping as an extension of health, lifestyle, and taste. The UI evokes:
- **Trust & Assurance:** High-grade editorial clarity and structured architecture ensure users feel confident in produce freshness and fulfillment accuracy.
- **Warmth & Appetite:** Inviting accents and generous negative space make browse-and-discovery feel tactile, fresh, and welcoming rather than utilitarian.
- **Effortless Precision:** Fast, friction-free interactions across cart additions, unit selectors, and checkout flows.

### Design Movement
**Corporate Modernist with Warm Editorial Nuance.** The interface leverages structured architectural grids, quiet layered card surfaces, deliberate typographic hierarchy, and micro-interactions that feel crisp and weighted. Surfaces remain calm in cool off-white and pure white, allowing product photography and focused orange accents to direct purchasing intent.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of maritime prestige, culinary energy, and clinical cleanliness.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary Navy (`#142B4A`):** Anchors brand presence, key transactional triggers, active navigation items, badges, and high-emphasis controls. Communicates stability and heritage.
- **Warm Orange (`#FF9F43`):** Reserved exclusively for promotional value—flash deals, discount chips, harvest tags, countdown timers, and urgent basket highlights. Used sparingly to preserve visual impact.
- **Dark Slate (`#263449`):** Primary text and icon color, delivering clear legibility without the harshness of pure black.
- **Muted Slate (`#5A6B82`):** Secondary text, unit weights, metadata, labels, and inactive icon states.
- **Canvas & Surface Layering:**
  - Base viewport: `#F7F9FC` (Cool Off-White) provides depth behind cards.
  - Interactive cards & sheets: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Pure White).
  - Outlines & dividing borders: `#E2E8F0` (light dividers) and `#CBD5E1` (focused structural boundaries).

## Typography

Manrope is utilized universally across headlines, body copy, and micro-labels to maintain structural clarity and modern geometric balance.

### Application Rules
- **Display & Headlines:** Set in bold and extra-bold weights with subtle negative tracking. Used for seasonal collection hero banners, aisle categories, and editorial recipe showcases.
- **Body:** Retains a tall x-height and generous line spacing for product descriptions, ingredient lists, and nutritional manifests.
- **Labels & Numbers:** Bold, high-tracking numbers are paired with currency markers to provide unambiguous pricing cues on crowded catalog screens.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an adaptable 12-column fluid grid system that flexes into compact single-column structures on handheld viewports.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and a max canvas width of `1360px` centered within a `3rem` boundary margin. Catalog views present 4 to 5 cards per row.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `2rem` side margins. Catalogs transition to 3 cards per row.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `0.75rem` (12px) gutters and `1rem` edge margins. Product grids shift to a 2-column layout or horizontal carousel scrolls.

### Spacing Principles
Component internals and relational elements adhere strictly to an 8px base rhythm:
- `space-xs` (4px): Inset badges, price-unit relations, tag icons.
- `space-sm` (8px): Form input padding, button text-icon separation, counter pills.
- `space-md` (16px): Card internal padding, standard form field vertical gap.
- `space-lg` (24px): Card headers, drawer headers, category section headers.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section-to-section layout spacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on quiet ambient shadows paired with crisp border containment to preserve an immaculate, clean store aesthetic.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F7F9FC` background. Never receives drop shadows.
- **Tier 1 (Cards, Tables, Shelves):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` solid `#E2E8F0` border and an ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(20, 43, 74, 0.05)`.
- **Tier 2 (Hovered Cards & Dropdowns):** Subtle lift using `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(20, 43, 74, 0.08)` and border shift to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Cart, Sticky Filter Bar, Sheets):** High tactile presence using `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(20, 43, 74, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, modern geometric rounding to produce an approachable yet precise grocery environment.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Base Components (`rounded`, 8px):** Applied to product images, regular buttons, standard text fields, search bars, and table cells.
- **Structural Modules (`rounded-lg`, 16px):** Applied to product listing cards, recipe cards, cart drawers, and promotional modals.
- **Pill Elements (Fully Rounded):** Reserved exclusively for dynamic status chips, promotional tags (`-20% OFF`), floating basket quantity badges, and filter category bubbles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#142B4A` with `#FFFFFF` text. Solid, authoritative, `8px` corner radius. Interactive state shifts to dark slate `#0E1E34`.
- **Secondary / Action:** Outlined `1.5px` solid `#142B4A` with navy text and transparent background. Shifts to subtle navy tint `#142B4A08` on hover.
- **Accent Deal Trigger:** Filled `#FF9F43` with `#142B4A` bold text for immediate conversion triggers like "Claim Offer" or "Flash Buy".

### Product Cards
- **Construction:** `#FFFFFF` background, `16px` radius, subtle `1px` border in `#E2E8F0`. 
- **Content Hierarchy:** High-resolution product visual centered, followed by origin/organic tag, bold product name in `#263449`, quantity/weight specification in `#5A6B82`, and bottom row split between bold pricing and an integrated stepper button.

### Quantity Stepper (Add to Cart)
- Default state: Compact navy icon button with plus symbol.
- Active state: Expands horizontally into a pill container with a decrement button, live unit quantity count, and an increment button. Background uses `#F7F9FC` with a `#CBD5E1` outline.

### Promotional Chips & Deal Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) tags resting atop card corners.
- Deal badges feature vibrant `#FF9F43` background with high-contrast `#142B4A` bold typography.
- Standard organic or regional certifications utilize `#F7F9FC` background with `#5A6B82` text and `#E2E8F0` border.

### Input Fields & Search
- Background: `#FFFFFF` encased in `1px` `#CBD5E1` border with an `8px` corner radius.
- Focus State: Smooth glow outline in `#142B4A` with a subtle offset ring (`#142B4A15`).
- Search bar incorporates a leading icon in `#5A6B82` and an inline trailing chip for delivery zip code selection.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Custom controls styled with `1.5px` border in `#CBD5E1` on `8px` (checkbox) and circular (radio) foundations.
- Active Selection: Filled solid `#142B4A` with a crisp `#FFFFFF` indicator checkmark.
